01 — Extraction

20 BARS.
REAL CREMA.

Espresso is pressure. The UNO's 20-bar pump forces water through the puck at café pressure, extracting the oils and body pod machines leave behind — finishing every shot with a thick, hazelnut crema.

02 — Dual boiler

BREW AND STEAM.
SAME MOMENT.

Single-boiler machines make you wait — shot first, steam later, coffee cooling in between. The UNO runs separate boilers for brewing and steam, so the milk is ready when the shot is. Lattes and cappuccinos in one pass, at drinking temperature.

03 — Control

ONE TOUCH.
NO DIALS TO LEARN.

An LCD touch panel with digital temperature display runs the whole machine: single shot, double shot, steam, hot water. Pick your drink, press once, watch it pour. Auto shut-off handles the rest.
